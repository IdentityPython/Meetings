IdPy TECHNICAL WORKING MEETINGS: 2026 Technical Re-engagement
===================================================

In mid 2026, members of the IdPy community engaged to re-establish the IdPy project which had been dormant. In June of that year, a [Town Hall Meeting](../2026-06-17%20IdPY%20Town%20Hall) was held to introduce this effort, and two weeks later, members of the technical community gathered for a [Technical Re-Kickoff Meeting](../2026-06-29%20IdPy%20Technical%20Re-Kickoff) to review a [Technical Re-kickoff charter](../2026-06-26%20DRAFT%20IdPY%20Technical%20Transition%20Charter.pdf) and establish project workstreams for a six-month effort. Notes and artifacts of this group's work will be stored in this folder.
